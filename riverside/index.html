How Drying Works on a Riverside Water Damage Job

A wet building left alone does dry eventually, but far too slowly. Left alone, water trapped in drywall, wood framing and subfloor can stay for weeks, which is long enough for mold and warping. On each Riverside job, our task is to make that happen quickly and under control.

Extraction is always step one, since pumping and vacuuming remove water much faster than any drying machine. Then air movers are set along the walls to push a fast stream of air across wet surfaces and lift moisture out of them. Dehumidifiers pull that moisture out of the air and send it down a drain, which keeps the room dry enough for evaporation to continue. Without the dehumidifiers, the air movers would just move damp air around.

We take moisture readings every day and compare them with a dry part of the same building. For most Riverside homes the target is met in 3 to 5 days, though hardwood, plaster and other dense materials need more time. The equipment comes out when the numbers say so, not when the calendar does.

Simple Ways to Keep a Riverside Home Dry

Most of the water damage we see in Riverside homes started with something small that gave warning first. A few minutes of checking a couple of times a year catches most of it.

Begin at the hoses. Washer, dishwasher, toilet and refrigerator lines made of plain rubber age badly, and swapping them for braided steel is a cheap fix. Look under every sink for stains, swollen cabinet floors or a musty smell. Check the water heater for rust at the base and drain a little from the tank once a year to clear sediment. Clean gutters and downspouts that discharge well away from the house keep rain out of the foundation.

Find the main water shutoff now, and show every adult in the home how to close it. Leak sensors placed by the water heater, washer and under sinks cost little and will alert you early. A stain that spreads or a floor that feels soft is worth a call, and Riverside homeowners are welcome to ask us about it.

Water Damage We See Most in Riverside

In Riverside, the same handful of problems accounts for nearly all the water damage we see. Knowing them makes it easier to catch trouble while it is still a small repair.

Pipes: When water freezes inside a line it can crack the metal or plastic, then pour out as soon as things warm up. Depending on the season, the plumbing most at risk runs through outside walls, attics and garages without heat. In older homes, aging joints and tired connectors sometimes fail with no cold snap involved. Your first move is closing the main water valve, since a broken pipe floods room after room fast.

Appliances: Washing machines, dishwashers and refrigerator ice makers all depend on small hoses that wear out. A water heater near the end of its life can leak from the tank bottom or fail all at once. We see many of these in Riverside homes where the unit sits upstairs or over a finished room.

Overflows: It takes only a few minutes for a blocked toilet or a forgotten faucet to flood a bathroom. The water often runs under baseboards and through the ceiling of the room below. If the overflow came from the toilet bowl or a drain, treat it as contaminated and keep children and pets away.

Roof: Missing shingles, cracked flashing and clogged gutters let rain into the attic. The stain on your ceiling usually shows up well after the insulation above it is soaked. The crew from Sunnyvale Restoration can dry the attic and ceiling, but the roof itself needs a repair to stop the leak.

Basement: In homes that have a basement, groundwater pushes through foundation cracks and floor joints when the soil is saturated. A sump pump that loses power or jams during a heavy rain lets the pit overflow. Carpet, wall studs and drywall wick moisture upward, which is why finished lower levels take the worst of it.

Mold: Mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours on materials that stay wet. Many mold jobs we handle in Riverside began with an old leak that was wiped up but never dried inside the wall. A musty smell, peeling paint or dark spotting near a previous repair is worth having checked.
