Sump Pump Failure Cleanup Help in Sunnyvale

A sump pump has one job, and it tends to fail at the moment it is needed most. During heavy rain the water table climbs and fills the pit, at which point an outage, a stuck float or an overwhelmed pump stops the pumping. From there it tops the edge of the basin and runs out over the slab. Nothing stops it until some pump is working again.

This water is not clean. It has come through soil and often carries silt, bacteria and whatever was in the drain tile, so we treat it as Category 2 at best and Category 3 if it has sat or mixed with sewage. Cleanup is different as a result, with wet drywall, carpet pad and other porous materials generally removed and the rest cleaned and disinfected ahead of drying.

First Five Minutes

What to Do Right Now

  1. Keep Out of the BasementNever wade into basement water before the power has been cut. The furnace, wall outlets or the cord of the pump itself could be in contact with it.
  2. Turn Off the Basement CircuitsTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. Call the utility when the panel itself sits in the flooded area.
  3. Look at the Pump From a DistanceLook to see whether the pump came unplugged, its outlet tripped or the float is pinned against the pit wall. Keep your hands out of the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyIf going down cannot be avoided, put on gloves and rubber boots, leave pets and children upstairs, and wash up after handling wet items.
  5. Move Valuables and CallCarry dry valuables upstairs, photograph the water level and the pump, then call us. Holding off until the basement empties is not the answer.
Will Insurance Pay?

Insurance and Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

This is one of the most misunderstood claims. A standard homeowners policy usually excludes water that backs up or overflows from a sump, and flood insurance is a separate policy with its own rules. Unless you bought an endorsement for sump overflow or water backup there is often no coverage, and such endorsements are capped. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. To keep the claim file complete, our team documents the damage, the failed pump and the height of the water.

The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • Discharge piping that has come apart, been flattened or plugged up outdoors.
  • A failed check valve that lets pumped water run back into the pit.
  • Undersized equipment that falls behind when inflow is heavy.
  • Dirt and small rocks washed into the basin that lock up the pump.
  • An old motor that burned out after years of cycling on and off.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Furnace and Water Heater

Sump pits tend to be located near the home's mechanical equipment. Water reaching burners, controls or wiring can ruin them, and each appliance must be inspected before it is used again.

Wall Framing

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. Since it is contaminated water, we remove the wet bottom portion instead of drying it.

Carpet and Flooring

There is no safe way to clean pad and carpet that sat in groundwater, so both are removed. Silty water also gets caught below vinyl plank and laminate, which normally means lifting them.

How We Fix It

How We Handle Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

1

Get the Water Out

Our crew arrives with extractors and pumps, which means your dead sump is not needed. Low spots, the area by the pit and the rest of the floor are cleared of water and silt.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Clean and Sanitize

An antimicrobial product goes on after the concrete, the studs and any kept belongings have been washed. This happens before drying so contaminants are not blown around the basement.

4

Dry and Confirm

Dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture meter readings on the framing and concrete return to normal, typically 3 to 5 days. Before we pull our equipment, the pit should have a pump that works.
