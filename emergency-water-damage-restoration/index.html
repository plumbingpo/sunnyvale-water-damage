How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
