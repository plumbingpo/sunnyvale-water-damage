How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Sunnyvale

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Rain blown in around door frames, window frames or openings in the wall
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
