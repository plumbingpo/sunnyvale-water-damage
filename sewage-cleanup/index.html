Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in Sunnyvale, Sunnyvale Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
