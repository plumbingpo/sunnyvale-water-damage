Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. Day or night, 24/7, Sunnyvale Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Sunnyvale to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Sunnyvale

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
